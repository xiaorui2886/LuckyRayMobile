# ADR-0003 — metadata-consistent seal, revision 2026-09-24.2

Revision `2026-09-24.1` and its published digest remain immutable at commit `8e28e6a65f08714dc9ce680a61db1cd773bdf325`. That bundle introduced the source-precision and bounded-search semantics in ADR-0002, but its machine operation inventory and versioned Schema `$id` still carried the prior revision string.

This new seal aligns the manifest, operation inventory, Schema identifier, fixture, golden catalog and production catalog at `2026-09-24.2`. It adds verifier assertions for those identities. There is **no DTO, route, authorization, source, search or Content V1 semantic change** from `2026-09-24.1`; clients that already consumed `.1` need only replace the canonical bundle and verify this revision's digest. Do not edit the old vendor bundle in place without updating its seal.
